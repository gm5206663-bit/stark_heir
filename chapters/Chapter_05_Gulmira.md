*PANEL ONE — THE COLOR OF A VOW*

The workshop did not go to bed with the city.

*PANEL TWO — GULMIRA*

At the northern crest of the province the road broke out of Gulmira as interrupted squiggles in the morning's beige — Jeeps, donkeys, an improvised schoolbus bucking against the dust-crusted corners. Ten Rings harbor-marked approaches everywhere along the outside-eaves of town, armed sufficient to resist anything the small air-pasture trained might be.

Men were driving women out of houses. Old women, first — the imprecise tyranny of fate in a tank's steering — too slow for utilities; children miscarrying themselves forward where fathers fought to pry them back in fire-hardened epidemics of muscle- applause.

One father — the man was a shopkeeper, had someone's ribbon in his pocket — driven to his knees in the dirt, flagging down enormity; the leader — Raza's officer, the man with the absolute gun, scorn through Mirage who had personal language like a rolled whisper —

"Get them back," Tony said — to the registers of language, any language, translated by the ancestor-caused adaptive sunshine: — then the tougher language from the suit's resonant modulation, loud at the chamber tone, hand raised against impermanence: — and the first rocket came out of the alley —

— but Tony did not dodge. The armor absorbed it. One staggered step toward the dust, and the shoulder-mounts rose and answered: micro-missiles left his shoulder in dry silver volleys, and the flak bed went up behind its own sandbags, and the gun trucks one after another learned what a whisper off a tongue looks like when it arrives hot.

The tank came up behind.

Armor weighs what armor weighs — and the turret delivered the whole argument on-target: Tony slammed down through a courtyard wall and unrolled lengthwise across the dust of someone's lost courtyard — and got up anyway. The villagers had run an instant past their astonishment long enough to fray direction — he gave them ten seconds, clearing part company with his gently wrought standard: *Still standing.*

He walked straight toward the tank, sunlight sliding off the red-gold plate, and when its gun fired he came through the concussion and laid the palm of one gauntlet against the tank's own muzzle and put a repulsor charge home until the machine's arithmetic went terminally hollow.

The tank exploded behind him.

Yinsen's last wish — child of no one's math — had put down as post-script: *don't waste your life.* The tank went up compressed behind Tony Stark, who was already outbound for the border and the sea-road home. Call him armed; the village called him saved. Either way, the children of Gulmira ate bread tonight that the rattled world only heard about tomorrow.

"JARVIS," he said to no one, or himself, or the sky. "File that. In the register for *goodness-noted-sides.*"

"Filed, sir," JARVIS said, for the lonely sea where every pilot's conscience comes home. "May I recommend the *return* path. This broadcast has reached a certain threshold of riot."

There was never any doubt. The solar panel of Malibu, the tuxedoed boy rooms and the pancakes, the memory that fell in Januaries of ordinary Drake style: everything, afterward, was only so that every found person could go home.
