## P3 — DREAMS/FEELINGS ≠ PROOF (sibling canon guard, verbatim)
"OC deduces NOTHING before evidence (dreams ≠ proof)." **Dual violation:** (a) my own bible §7 said slumber "dreams that later come true" — codified at foundation level; (b) Ch1's 2am dread + dawn news = an on-page dream/feeling proven true, then used to power guilt. **Law:** foreknowledge-type impressions must stay UNCERTIFIED for the CHARACTERS on page.
